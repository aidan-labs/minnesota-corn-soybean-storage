# Minnesota Corn Harvest Planning

**Decision question:** Which county in our service area should our grain elevator contact first to plan for harvest intake?

**Data source:** [USDA NASS Quick Stats](https://quickstats.nass.usda.gov/)

**Data I’ll use:** Annual corn grain production in total bushels for selected Minnesota counties, using the same year for each county. I’ll rank the counties by production to decide which one to contact first.
