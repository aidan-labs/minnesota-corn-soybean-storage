Business interpretation

What this means for MCSS

MCSS stores farmers' grain and prepares it for release; it does not own the grain or arrange transportation. Statewide production can inform broad capacity discussions, but operational delivery decisions require facility-level inventory, farmer release requests, and pickup records.
