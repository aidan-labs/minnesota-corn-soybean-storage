What Does This Mean for Supply Chains?

Changes in corn production can affect grain elevator storage requirements, truck scheduling, and transportation capacity.

Grain elevators can use historical production trends as one input when planning future operations. However, statewide organic production alone cannot predict demand at an individual elevator.

Data Source and Limitations

Source: USDA National Agricultural Statistics Service (NASS), Quick Stats.

The dataset reports Minnesota organic grain corn production in bushels. It contains selected years from 2008 through 2025, not every year.

The 2008 and 2014 figures combine USDA-certified and exempt organic production. Other displayed years represent USDA-certified production. Values marked (D) were withheld and are excluded. These category differences limit direct comparisons.
